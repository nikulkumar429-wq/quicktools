Free Online Tools at QuickTools

QuickTools is a collection of simple and free online tools for everyday tasks. The website provides browser-based tools for compressing images, resizing images, calculating age and converting common units.

Our goal is to make useful tools easy to access without requiring complicated software or installations.

Free Image Compressor

The QuickTools Image Compressor helps reduce the size of image files. Smaller image files can be easier to upload, share and use on websites.

Select an image and click the Compress Image button. The tool creates a compressed JPEG image that you can save to your device.

Free Image Resizer

The QuickTools Image Resizer allows you to change the width and height of an image. Enter your desired dimensions and the tool creates a resized image.

Free Age Calculator

The QuickTools Age Calculator calculates age from a date of birth. It provides the result in years, months and days.

Free Unit Converter

The Unit Converter supports common conversions including kilometers to miles, miles to kilometers, kilograms to pounds, pounds to kilograms, centimeters to meters and meters to centimeters.

How to Use QuickTools

How to compress an image

Open the Image Compressor, choose an image from your device, then click Compress Image. After processing, use the download button to save the compressed image.

How to resize an image

Open the Image Resizer, choose an image, enter the required width and height, then click Resize Image.

How to calculate age

Open the Age Calculator, select your date of birth and click Calculate Age to see your age in years, months and days.

How to convert units

Enter a number, choose the conversion type and click Convert to get the converted value.
